Privacy Policy

Rocktier PDF Squeeze — Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Overview

Rocktier PDF Squeeze is a desktop PDF compression application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we handle your files, and what third-party services we use.

2. No Data Collection

Rocktier PDF Squeeze does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage statistics, analytics, crash reports, or any other information. The application runs entirely on your local machine.

3. File Handling

  • Local processing only: All PDF files you select are processed locally on your computer. No files are uploaded to any server.
  • No persistence: Your selected files are not saved or remembered between sessions.
  • Temporary files: During compression, temporary files are created in your system's cache directory. These are cleaned up automatically after compression completes, or upon cancellation.
  • No cloud storage: The application has no integration with cloud storage services.

4. Third-Party Services

Rocktier PDF Squeeze bundles a local copy of Ghostscript, an open-source PDF processing engine. Ghostscript runs entirely on your machine and does not communicate with any external service.

No other third-party services, SDKs, analytics frameworks, or telemetry libraries are included in the application.

5. Updates

Rocktier PDF Squeeze does not include an automatic update mechanism. Updates are distributed exclusively through the Microsoft Store and other official distribution channels. You have full control over when and whether to install updates.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the change. Since the application has no online connectivity, you can always find the latest version at this URL.
